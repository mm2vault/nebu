Questions

Asked, answered.

What is Nebu?

A link-in-bio page you design yourself. Pick a name, add your links, choose a background and card style, and share nebu.app/yourname anywhere.

How are UIDs assigned?

In the order accounts are created. UIDs 1 to 250 get the OG badge on their profile.

Are the analytics accurate?

Known bots are ignored, your own visits are not counted, and repeat refreshes from the same visitor within 30 minutes count once.

Can I change my username?

Yes, from the Customize tab, as long as the new name is free.

Can I delete my account?

Anytime from the dashboard. Your page, links and stats are removed immediately.
